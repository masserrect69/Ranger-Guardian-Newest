# Ford OEM public reference notes — Ranger 3.2 / PX MkII

## 3.2 L Duratorq/Puma engine — Ford Owner Manual data
Public Ford owner-manual content for the 3.2L Duratorq-TDCi (148 kW / 200 PS) lists:
- displacement: 3,198 cm³
- power: 147 kW (200 PS) at 3,000 rpm
- maximum torque: 470 Nm at 1,750–2,500 rpm
- continuous engine speed: 4,500 rpm
- maximum intermittent engine speed: 4,900 rpm
- idle: 700 ±150 rpm
- induction: turbocharged and intercooled
- firing order: 1-2-4-5-3
- compression ignition

Market/variant applicability must still be checked before treating a page as exact for the configured Australian 2018 vehicle.

## Capacities — public Ford Ranger Owner Manual content
A Ford Ranger owner-manual capacities page covering the 3.2L Duratorq lists engine oil with filter 9.75 L, automatic transmission dry 10.5 L / wet 9 L, and 3.2L automatic cooling system 13.5 L. These are useful reference anchors, but fluid specification and exact variant/year applicability must be verified from the vehicle's applicable owner/workshop documentation before service.

## DPF use guidance — Ford owner-manual family
Ford owner-manual content states that the DPF requires periodic regeneration and gives driving guidance for journeys that otherwise inhibit regeneration: more favorable higher-speed main-road/freeway driving for at least 20 minutes, avoiding prolonged idling and maintaining a suitable gear around 1,500–3,000 rpm. Ranger Brain must verify exact model-year applicability before presenting this as a specific 2018 procedure.

## Body and Equipment Mounting Manuals — Ford Australia
Ford Australia publicly lists PX Ranger MkII BEMMs for 2015MY and 2019MY. These are valuable for body/electrical architecture, battery/alternator, fuses/relays, routing and modification constraints, but they are not substitutes for the powertrain workshop manual.
