# High-value workshop facts for Ford Guardian

These are retrieval anchors distilled from the uploaded workshop manual. They are not a substitute for
the full procedure. Always retrieve the source page before giving a repair instruction.

## 3.2L Duratorq/Puma engine
Source: PDF p.1256 and p.1263.
- Five-cylinder inline 3.2L Duratorq-TDCi/Puma.
- Displacement: 3198 cc.
- Firing order: 1-2-4-5-3.
- Compression ratio: 15.6:1.
- Torque: 470 Nm between 1750 and 2500 rpm.
- Idle speed: 700 rpm.
- Service engine-oil fill including filter: 9.8 L.
- Engine overview states cylinder 1 is at the timing-chain end.

## Turbocharger / charge-air diagnosis
Sources: PDF pp.1513-1519, 1594-1603.
- Variable-vane turbo diagnosis includes visual inspection of intake/charge-air components,
  turbo oil feed/return, wiring/connectors and the variable-vane actuator.
- For excessive turbo noise, the diagnostic flow checks rotor-vane damage, foreign material,
  free rotor rotation/bearing movement and oil-supply restriction.
- Poor performance should include charge-air-cooler and charge-air-pipe leak/obstruction checks.
- The CAC leak test procedure pressurizes to 150 kPa (22 psi); pressure loss above 10 kPa
  (1.5 psi) per minute calls for CAC replacement.
- Intake/turbo openings must be protected from debris; small particles can damage compressor vanes.

## 3.2L EGR
Sources: PDF pp.1555, 1563-1570.
- The built-from-17-08-2015 3.2L EGR system includes an EGR valve, cooler, bypass valve/actuator,
  inlet/outlet coolant hoses and EGR outlet tube.
- The EGR valve removal procedure specifies new gaskets during installation.
- Do not infer P0404 pinpoint-test logic unless the exact diagnostic page is retrieved; this bundle
  contains procedures but not every DTC-specific flow in the same section.

## DPF / exhaust
Sources: PDF pp.1931-1937.
- The 3.2L DPF exhaust layout identifies the DPF, differential-pressure sensor/lines and EGT sensors.
- Generic DPF operation in the bundle states the PCM uses differential pressure and temperature inputs
  to decide when regeneration is required.
- Regeneration is described as raising DPF exhaust temperature above about 550 C so soot burns,
  while ash remains trapped.
- A blocked DPF may be associated with excessive soot production, repeated failed regeneration or
  related-system faults such as an EGR valve stuck open, a charge-air leak or injector faults.
- Treat Euro-6/SCR content as market-specific and not automatically applicable to the Australian vehicle.

## 6R80
Sources: PDF pp.1642-1643, 1704-1709.
- Transmission: 6R80, floor shift.
- Specified operating temperature: 80-110 C.
- ATF specification: WSS-M2C938-A / Motorcraft MERCON LV.
- Capacity shown: 9 L wet, 10.5 L dry.
- Fluid change interval shown in this manual: 240,000 km.
- Preliminary diagnosis starts by reproducing the concern under its real conditions, checking fluid
  level/condition, scanning for DTCs and noting temperature/load/shift dependencies.
- Low fluid can cause poor engagement/slip/failure; overfill can aerate fluid and cause erratic pressure.
- The 6R80 uses an internal thermal bypass that can stop cooler flow below normal operating temperature.
